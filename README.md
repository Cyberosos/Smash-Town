# Smash Town

A task app for ADHD brains. Every task you finish grows a colorful town.

- **Quick tasks**: smash one to get a burst, a sound, and a surprise flower or house.
- **Tiny steps (optional)**: break a big task into steps. Each step drops a new floor on a tower.
- **Monthly town**: each day is a fenced plot. Quiet days get a sleeping cat. On the 1st, the town moves to Town History and a fresh month begins.
- **Just 5 minutes**: a timer that rewards you for starting.

Open it on your phone and choose **Add to Home Screen** to install it. Your town is saved on your device and works offline.
